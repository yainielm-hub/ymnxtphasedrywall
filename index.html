What We Do

Our Services

📐
Steel & Wood Framing

Interior wall and ceiling framing using wood or metal studs. Precise, code-compliant layouts for new builds, additions, and basement finishes.

🏗️
Drywall Installation

Full drywall hanging for new construction and renovations — walls, ceilings, bulkheads, and soffits. Residential and commercial, done right.

🎨
Taping & Mudding

Expert joint taping, bedding, and finishing coats. Seamless, level-5 smooth walls and ceilings ready for paint — every time.

🔧
Patch Work & Repairs

Holes, cracks, water damage, and impact repairs fixed to perfection. We match existing textures so patches are completely invisible.

⬛
Drop Ceilings & T-Bar

Suspended T-bar grid ceiling systems for commercial and residential spaces. Clean lines, acoustic tiles, and concealed mechanicals.

🌀
Popcorn Removal

Remove outdated acoustic popcorn ceilings cleanly and efficiently, leaving a smooth modern surface ready for painting or new texture.

🏛️
California Ceilings

Application and removal of California skip-trowel ceiling texture. Sprayed or hand-applied for a beautiful, consistent finish.

✨
Texture & Spray Finishes

Smooth, orange peel, knockdown, skip trowel, and custom spray textures — every finish matched perfectly to your style.

🧱
Insulation Installation

Batt and rigid insulation in wall and ceiling cavities before drywall. Improve energy efficiency and soundproofing throughout your home.

🔇
Soundproofing

Resilient channels, acoustic insulation, and double-layer drywall for superior sound control — home theatres, offices, and multi-family units.

📦
Bulkheads & Soffits

Custom bulkheads and soffits to conceal ductwork, pipes, and beams — finished seamlessly into your walls and ceilings.

🖌️
Interior Painting

From primer to final colour — walls, ceilings, and trim painted to a professional standard. The perfect finishing touch.
